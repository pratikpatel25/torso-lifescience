Torso Lifesciences Pvt. Ltd. is a dedicated pharmaceutical formulation developer and global supplier focusing on the treatment and care of kidney-related medical states.

Since our inception, we have stood at the forefront of nephrology and renal care solutions, introducing a diverse portfolio of specialized therapies designed to improve the life quality of kidney disease and transplant patients.

Headquartered in the commercial hub of Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, Torso Lifesciences is built upon values of excellence, safety, and patient focus. We understand the physical, emotional, and economic challenges faced by patients undergoing chronic kidney disease (CKD) therapies, dialysis, or organ transplants. By providing access to high-standard, bio-equivalent medicines at affordable rates, we help bridge the gaps in global renal care.
