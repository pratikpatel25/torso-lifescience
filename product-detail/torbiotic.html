Torbiotic

Indication & Usage

Torbiotic Capsules are made up of Prebiotics and Probiotics. Prebiotics are indigestible plant fibers that encourage the growth of beneficial bacteria in the gut. By increasing the production of short-chain fatty acids (such as butyrate) and promoting Vitamin K production, prebiotics contribute to a healthier gut environment. Probiotics are live microorganisms that, when consumed in adequate amounts, provide health benefits to the host. Probiotics help to restore microbial balance and potentially inhibit the production of uremic toxins and toxic metabolites and also reduce inflammatory markers (such as Interleukin-6) and decrease symptoms of uremia in CKD

Composition

Calcium Acetate Tablets USP 667mg

Mode of Action

1. Reduction of Uremic Toxins: By reducing the levels of uremic toxins like indoxyl sulfate and p-cresyl sulfate, which are produced by gut bacteria from dietary proteins. Probiotics can reduce the production of these toxins by altering the gut microbiota composition and reducing the activity of toxin-producing bacteria, 2. Gut Microbiota Modulation: restore a healthier gut microbiome, potentially improving gut health and reducing systemic inflammation, 3. Anti-inflammatory Effects: exert anti-inflammatory effects, by modulating the immune response and decrease the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines, 4. Enhancement of Gut Barrier Function Probiotics enhance the gut barrier function, reducing intestinal permeability and preventing systemic inflammation and endotoxemia, 5. Improvement of Lipid Metabolism: influence lipid metabolism by promoting the excretion of bile acids and reducing cholesterol levels, 6. Reduction of Oxidative Stress : reduce oxidative stress by increase the levels of antioxidant enzymes and reduce the production of reactive oxygen species (ROS), which can protect kidney cells from oxidative damage.

Indications

1. Reduction in uremic toxins and inflammatory markers, 2. Decrease in serum CRP and reduction of gastrointestinal symptoms, 3. Reduction in BUN, increased quality of life, and absence of adverse effects in patients with Stage 3 & 4 CKD, 4. Decrease in PCS and favorable changes to fecal bacteria of patients with CKD stage 4 & 5, 5. Decreased cardiovascular risk 6. Better blood sugar management, 7. Nourishing the beneficial bacteria in the gut.

Dosage

1-3 Capsules per day. This information is for the registered medical practitioners only. Consult your medical practitioner before consuming the products.
