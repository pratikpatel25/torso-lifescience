Renoace

Indication & Usage

Renoace, is a combination of Taurine 500mg and N-acetylcysteine 150mg. Renoace prevents or attenuates glomerular damage in diabetes. N-Acetylcysteine and Taurine work by slowing down the process of kidney cell damage and improving kidney function. Renoace particularly beneficial in preventing the progression of microalbuminuria (the presence of small amounts of albumin in the urine) in patients with diabetic kidney disease.

Composition

Taurine 500 mg + Acetylcysteine 150 mg Tablets

Mode of Action

NAC is an antioxidant and a precursor to glutathione, a powerful antioxidant produced by the body. It works by slowing down the process of kidney cell damage and improving kidney function. Specifically, NAC has been found to be beneficial in preventing the progression of microalbuminuria in patients with diabetic kidney disease. Microalbuminuria refers to the presence of small amounts of albumin (a protein) in the urine, which can be an early sign of kidney damage.
