Indication & Usage

Protein is an important nutrient that helps to repair and build body tissues, drives metabolic reactions, maintains pH and fluid balance, and keeps the immune system strong, also transports and stores nutrients and act as an energy source, plays a very crucial role for good health. Protein requirements for dialysis patients are increased due to several reasons: 1. Protein Loss During Dialysis, 2. Protein Loss During Dialysis, 3. Chronic inflammation, 4. Poor Appetite and Dietary Restrictions, 5. Poor Appetite and Dietary Restrictions, Also, Dialysis patients are at increased risk of infections and other complications, which can further increase protein needs. Body requires more protein to repair tissues and produce immune factors during these periods. Adequate protein intake is crucial for maintaining a good nutritional status, which is associated with better outcomes in dialysis patients.

Composition

For HD: 1.2g protein/kg body weight · For PD: 1.5g protein/kg body weight
